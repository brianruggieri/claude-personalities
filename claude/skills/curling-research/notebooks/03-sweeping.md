## Scope

Sweeping is the curling-specific act of brushing the ice surface ahead of a moving stone to alter its trajectory — increase distance, hold line, or (controversially) bend its path. This notebook collects peer-reviewed research on the physical mechanism of the sweep effect (heat-melting vs scratch theories), instrumented-broom biomechanics (force, cadence, stroke geometry), athlete physiology and fatigue under sweeping load, the regulatory history of the 2015–2016 "Broomgate" controversy and the World Curling Federation broom directive, the BalancePlus EQ / WCF-approved fabric standardization, the 2026 Sweeping Technique Policy, two-sweeper coordination and single-sweeper tactics, sweeping calls and team communication, and the rule variants that govern who may sweep where (mixed doubles, wheelchair, behind the tee line). **Excludes:** generic stone-motion / curl-mechanism physics that is not invoked by sweeping (Notebook 2 — Physics), broom equipment as a regulatory catalog of approved gear and cosmetic spec (Notebook 7 — Equipment), and ice technician / pebbling craft that is not about sweeping itself (Notebook 8 — Ice Technician).

## Coverage Outline

- Sweep-effect mechanism: heat-melting / liquid-film theories vs. scratch / abrasion theories
- Instrumented brushes (Marmo–Buckingham sweep ergometer, SmartBroom, USask stone-throwing rig)
- Force, cadence, stroke length, push vs. pull asymmetry, peak vertical loads (~450 N elite)
- Two-sweeper coordination: inside/outside positioning, 80/20 contribution split, the 18-inch pre-warm rule
- Single-sweeper tactic for directional control
- Press sweeping vs. fast strokes; "bunny-hop" technique
- 2015–2016 Broomgate: icePad directional fabric, Black Magic, ice damage at the Stu Sells Tankard
- Athlete-led gentleman's agreement and the November 2015 WCF moratorium
- May 2016 Sweeping Summit in Kemptville, Ontario (NRC of Canada testing protocol, 50+ brush heads, robots & GPS)
- Adoption of single Nylon Oxford 420D fabric standard for 2016–17
- BalancePlus EQ and WCF-approved faceplates as the post-Broomgate equipment baseline
- 2026 World Curling Sweeping Technique Policy (in-game umpire enforcement, prohibited single-stroke patterns)
- Sweeping calls: "hard," "hurry hard," "yup," "clean," "whoa," "off," "right off," and the volume/urgency channel
- Sweeping for line vs. sweeping for distance; brush angle (45° straightening, 90° "knifing")
- Mixed doubles sweeping (anywhere in front of tee for delivering team; only skip/vice of non-delivering team behind tee)
- Wheelchair curling: no sweeping at all (forfeit penalty); implications for delivery accuracy
- Sweeping physiology: heart rate, mechanical efficiency, fatigue across a 10-end game
- Curl-trajectory effects of sweeping vs. non-sweeping in published trajectory studies

## Sources (25)

### 1. Sweeping effects on curling and friction estimation
- **URL:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12283-024-00473-5
- **Type:** paper
- **Why it matters:** 2024 Sports Engineering paper that quantifies the friction reduction caused by sweeping and ties it to trajectory change — primary modern reference for any sim wanting to reproduce sweep-effect on stone path.

### 2. The Sports Science of Curling: A Practical Review (Bradley, 2009)
- **URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3761524/
- **Type:** paper
- **Why it matters:** Bradley's J Sports Sci Med review is the canonical "what we know about curling" survey covering sweeping physiology, biomechanics, and ice friction; cited by virtually every later sweeping paper.

### 3. Frictional Heat Generated by Sweeping in Curling and its Effect on Ice Friction (Marmo, Farrow, Buckingham, Blackford)
- **URL:** https://www.lanarkcurlingclub.org/wp-content/uploads/INF_Frictional-Heat-Generated-By-Sweeping-In-Curling_Paper.pdf
- **Type:** paper
- **Why it matters:** Foundational measurement of the temperature rise sweeping produces and the resulting friction drop — the empirical basis of the heat-melt sweeping theory.

### 4. Design and use of an instrumented curling brush (Marmo & Blackford)
- **URL:** https://www.researchgate.net/publication/245390090_Design_and_use_of_an_instrumented_curling_brush
- **Type:** paper
- **Why it matters:** Proc. IMechE Part L (2006) paper introducing the original "sweep ergometer" — the first quantitative instrument for measuring vertical load, stroke rate, and brush-head kinematics; spec sheet for any sim modeling sweep-power.

### 5. Optimising Sweeping Techniques for Olympic Curlers (Buckingham, Marmo, Blackford)
- **URL:** https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-0-387-45951-6_45
- **Type:** paper
- **Why it matters:** Engineering of Sport 6 chapter applying the sweep ergometer to Olympic-level athletes; reports peak ~450 N vertical loads and analyzes the technique-fatigue trade-off.

### 6. A Case Study on Curling Stone and Sweeping Effect According to Sweeping Conditions
- **URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7835804/

- **Why it matters:** Korean study isolating sweeping cadence and pressure conditions and measuring resulting stone displacement — useful empirical anchor for sweep-strength → distance-added mapping.

### 7. An examination of studies related to the sport of curling: a scoping review (Zacharias, Robak, Passmore, 2024)
