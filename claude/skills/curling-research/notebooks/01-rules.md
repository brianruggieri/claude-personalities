# Curling Rules & Competition Structure

> Part of the curling-research skill for ~/git/curling. Paste the URLs below into a NotebookLM notebook with this name.

## Scope

Authoritative source corpus for the formal rules of curling and the competition structures that surround them. Covers the World Curling rulebook (general play, mixed doubles, wheelchair, stick), national-member federation rule supplements (Curling Canada, USA Curling, Scottish Curling), competition policy and officiating manuals, sheet/house dimensional specifications, in-play rules (free guard zone and the five-rock variant, no-tick rule, hog-line release and electronic detection, burned/touched-moving stones, out-of-play, last-stone-draw and draw-shot challenge tiebreakers), thinking-time and timeout regulations, mixed-doubles power play and positional stones, wheelchair sweeping prohibition and delivery sticks, and competition formats (round robin, Page playoff, qualification points, Brier/Scotties, Worlds, Mixed Doubles Worlds, Olympic/Paralympic qualification, 2026-2030 cycle changes). **Excludes:** strategy/tactics and analytics (Notebook 5), shot taxonomy and named shot types (Notebook 4), broadcast presentation of timeclocks (Notebook 9), historical evolution of rules and curling lore (Notebook 10), sweeping biomechanics or broom regulations (Notebook 3), equipment specifications beyond rulebook references (Notebook 7).

## Sources (25)

### 1. World Curling — The Rules of Curling (2024 edition)
- **URL:** https://worldcurling.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Rules-2024.pdf
- **Type:** rulebook
- **Why it matters:** Single canonical PDF combining general team play, mixed doubles, wheelchair, and stick variants — the document USA Curling, Curling Canada, and most member associations defer to. Primary source of truth for every rule citation.

### 2. World Curling — Rules of Curling landing page
- **URL:** https://worldcurling.org/rules/
- **Type:** org-page
- **Why it matters:** Authoritative index for rule supplements, mid-cycle rule-change bulletins, and translation editions; canonical entry point when verifying that a cited rule is still current.

### 3. World Curling — Competition Policy & Procedure Manual (Oct 2024)
- **URL:** https://worldcurling.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Policy-Procedures-Manual.pdf
- **Type:** manual
- **Why it matters:** The "how the championships actually run" manual — pool seeding, tiebreak rules, DSC handling, pre-game protocols, conduct policy. The rulebook says *what* the rules are; this says *how competitions execute them*.

### 4. World Curling — Technical Officials Manual (2020)
- **URL:** https://worldcurling.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Technical-Officials-Manual.pdf
- **Type:** manual
- **Why it matters:** Officiating procedures, measurement protocols, timing-system operation, and infraction adjudication detail not present in the player-facing rulebook. Essential for accurately modelling violations and edge-case rulings.

### 5. World Curling — Major changes to competition structure for 2026-2030 Olympic cycle
- **URL:** https://worldcurling.org/2025/10/competition-structure-changes/
- **Type:** org-page
- **Why it matters:** Defines the new 13→18 team Worlds expansion, B/C division tiers, and qualification overhaul that supersede prior format references. Without this, any post-2026 competition modelling is wrong.

### 6. World Curling — Olympic Qualification page
- **URL:** https://worldcurling.org/olympic-qualification/
- **Type:** org-page
- **Why it matters:** Canonical Olympic Qualification Points table and slot-allocation procedure for Beijing→Milan→2030 cycle. Distinct from the Paralympic flow.

### 7. World Curling — Paralympic Qualification page
- **URL:** https://worldcurling.org/paralympic-qualification/
- **Type:** org-page
- **Why it matters:** Wheelchair-specific qualification slots (mixed team and mixed doubles), classification requirements, and host-country reallocation procedure — Paralympic flow differs materially from the Olympic flow.

### 8. World Curling — Rule violations clarification, Cortina 2026
- **URL:** https://worldcurling.org/2026/02/statement-rules-violations/
- **Type:** org-page
- **Why it matters:** Live worked example of how World Curling adjudicates a high-profile touched-moving-stone incident at the Olympics. Shows the rule applied in practice, not just text.

### 9. Curling Canada — Rules of Curling for General Play 2022-2026
- **URL:** https://www.curling.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Rule-Book-2022_General-Play_ENG_CC.pdf
- **Type:** rulebook
- **Why it matters:** National-member rulebook valid through Aug 2026 — the variant most North American club and bonspiel play uses. Diverges from WCF on a handful of points (no-tick, club-level provisions).

### 10. Curling Canada — Rules of Curling landing page
- **URL:** https://www.curling.ca/rules/
- **Type:** org-page
- **Why it matters:** Index into Curling Canada's rule supplements (officiated play, no-tick poster, summary of changes). Fastest route to the most recent CC interpretation bulletins.

### 11. Curling Canada — Time Clock Operator Reference Manual (Nov 2025)
- **URL:** https://www.curling.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Time-Clock-Operator-Manual.pdf
- **Type:** manual
- **Why it matters:** Operational source-of-truth for thinking-time mechanics, stop/start triggers, timeout handling, and the 4:30-per-stone fallback when a team runs out. Replaces stale older references.

### 12. Curling Canada — Trial "no-tick" rule (Oct 2022 announcement)
- **URL:** https://www.curling.ca/blog/2022/10/14/trial-no-tick-rule/
- **Type:** article
- **Why it matters:** First-party announcement defining the no-tick rule's exact text, scope, and trial conditions before its formal adoption — settles the question of *which* stones the no-tick clause applies to.

### 13. Curling Canada — Last Stone Draw Process
- **URL:** https://www.curling.ca/rules/last-stone-draw-process/
- **Type:** rulebook
- **Why it matters:** Authoritative LSD/DSC procedure: rotation requirements, measurement, discarded-high-LSD averaging for tiebreakers. The exact algorithm a sim must reproduce.

### 14. Curling Canada — Scotties and Brier Format and Qualification
- **URL:** https://www.curling.ca/events/competitor-information/scotties-and-brier-format-and-qualification/
- **Type:** org-page
- **Why it matters:** The 18-team / two-pool / six-team-playoff Canadian-championship format, including post-2024 elimination of tiebreakers — distinct from Worlds and from older Brier formats.

### 15. USA Curling — Adopts WCF Rules of Curling and Competition
- **URL:** https://www.usacurling.org/press-releases/wcfrulesofcurlingcompetition
- **Type:** org-page
- **Why it matters:** Documents the USCA→WCF rule alignment effective 2023-24, eliminating most US-specific rule divergence at the national-championship level. Critical for not chasing stale USCA-specific rule variants.

### 16. USA Curling — 2025-26 Championship Qualifying Procedures
- **URL:** https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5f34a2ea0d87a44cac1ebfb0/t/686433cd2edd0d7fafd0c886/1751397326350/2025-26+Qualifying+Procedures07012025.pdf
- **Type:** manual
- **Why it matters:** US-specific qualification flow (RRA playdowns, Club Nationals, Mixed, Senior, Junior pathways) that WCF rules don't cover. The "how does Team USA get to Worlds" answer.

### 17. Scottish Curling — Rule Book 2023-24
- **URL:** https://www.scottishcurling.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Rule-Book-2023-24_FINAL1.0.pdf
- **Type:** rulebook
- **Why it matters:** RCCC/Scottish Curling national rule book — the lineage closest to the original 1838 Grand Caledonian rules and a useful European-context counterpart to Curling Canada's variant.

### 18. Scottish Curling — The Five-Rock Rule: Your Questions Answered
- **URL:** https://www.scottishcurling.org/the-five-rock-rule-your-questions-answered/
- **Type:** article
- **Why it matters:** Plain-language federation Q&A that pinpoints the exact stone-count semantics ("prior to delivery of the sixth stone") that frequently get garbled in secondary sources.

### 19. Curling Canada Blog — Five-rock FGZ a positive change for curling (Thiessen, 2018)
- **URL:** https://www.curling.ca/blog/2018/06/15/thiessen-blog-five-rock-fgz-a-positive-change-for-curling/
- **Type:** article
- **Why it matters:** Federation-authored rationale for the 4→5 rock change with concrete tactical reasoning — the *why* behind the rule, useful when calibrating AI scoring/strategy weightings against intent.

### 20. SIRC — Sport Insight: The rule change that revolutionized curling
- **URL:** https://sirc.ca/articles/sport-insight-the-rule-change-that-revolutionized-curling/
- **Type:** article
- **Why it matters:** Credentialed sport-research center analysis with concrete pre/post-rule statistics (42%→57% guarded ends, 64%→42% fifth-stone hit rates). Empirical impact data, not opinion.

### 21. World Mixed Doubles Curling Championship (Wikipedia, canonical reference)
- **URL:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Mixed_Doubles_Curling_Championship
- **Type:** article
- **Why it matters:** Single up-to-date summary of the 20-team / two-pool-of-ten / crossover-to-semis format, championship history, and field composition. Best concise format reference; the one Wikipedia entry permitted under the quality bar.

### 22. World Curling — ACE & Company World Mixed Doubles Curling Championship 2026 event page
- **URL:** https://worldcurling.org/events/worldmixeddoubles/
- **Type:** org-page
- **Why it matters:** First-party event page documenting the live championship's executed format (Group A/B, advancement) — pairs with the Wikipedia entry for cross-verification of current-cycle details.

### 23. International Paralympic Committee — Wheelchair Curling sport page
- **URL:** https://www.paralympic.org/wheelchair-curling
- **Type:** org-page
- **Why it matters:** IPC-authoritative wheelchair-curling overview: classification (single class, lower-limb impairment), no-sweeping rule, delivery-stick mechanics, mixed-team requirement of both sexes on ice.

### 24. Eye on the Hog — Wikipedia
- **URL:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eye_on_the_Hog
- **Type:** article
- **Why it matters:** Comprehensive technical history of the electronic hog-line release sensor, including known malfunction incidents 2011-2021 and the Czech-developed 2026 successor. Single-source overview not replicated elsewhere.

### 25. World Curling — Building a Modern Curling Facility
- **URL:** https://worldcurling.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Building-a-Modern-Curling-Facility_ENG.pdf
- **Type:** manual
- **Why it matters:** Authoritative dimensional specifications for sheet length/width, house geometry, hog-line placement, hack positioning, and ice-plant requirements. The geometry source the simulation's `SheetGeometry` should ultimately trace back to.
